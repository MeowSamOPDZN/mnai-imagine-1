Try these first, they are the ten things Imagine 1 is trained on:
a photo of an airplane
a photo of an automobile
a photo of a bird
a photo of a cat
a photo of a deer
a photo of a dog
a photo of a frog
a photo of a horse
a photo of a ship
a photo of a truck

Anything outside those ten will come out as a blurry mix of them.
That is normal for a first 250M, it only knows what it was shown.
